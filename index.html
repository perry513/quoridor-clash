QuoridorClash

    Fan-made game based on the Quoridor rules. Not affiliated with Gigamic.

    How to play

    1. 1
      Race to the far sideBe the first hero to reach any square on the opposite edge. Your goal edge glows in your colour.
    2. 2
      One action per turnMove one square up, down, left or right, or build one wall.
    3. 3
      Walls block the wayEach wall is two squares long and sits in the gaps. Nobody can walk through it.
    4. 4
      Never trap anyoneEvery hero must always have some path to their goal. The game will not let you build a wall that seals someone in.
    5. 5
      Jump over heroesFace to face with another hero? Hop over them. If a wall or the board edge is behind them, step diagonally instead.
    6. 6
      Wall supply10 walls each with 2 players, 5 each with 4. When yours run out, you can only move.
